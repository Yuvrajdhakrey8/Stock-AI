DhakreyStock AI - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Commitment to User Privacy: DhakreyStock AI ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. We do not collect personal identifying information, phone numbers, contacts, bank credentials, or trading account passwords.

1. Information We Collect

DhakreyStock AI operates primarily as an analytical computation tool. Data handling is limited to:

  • Device & Usage Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and performance data automatically collected by underlying mobile OS platforms (such as app crashes, device model, and OS version).
  • Local Preferences: Your saved stock watchlists and user interface preferences are stored locally on your device via browser local storage and are never transferred to our private databases.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To provide free quantitative valuation tools and market data, DhakreyStock AI integrates verified third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob (Google Play Services): We display non-intrusive mobile banner and interstitial advertisements to support infrastructure costs. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (GAID) in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad personalization in your Android Device Settings.
  • Public Market Feeds (NSE / BSE / Yahoo Finance): Real-time price quotes, balance sheet ratios, and historical OHLC data are queried from publicly available financial data providers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All network communications between the DhakreyStock AI mobile application and our cloud processing backend (AWS API Gateway) are strictly transmitted over industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3) encryption. We do not maintain user profile databases or store unencrypted financial records.

4. Children's Privacy

DhakreyStock AI is intended for individuals of legal investing age. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

Regulatory & Financial Disclaimer (SEBI Compliance)

DhakreyStock AI provides algorithmic valuation estimates (DCF intrinsic fair value, Graham formula, technical momentum indicators, and IPO subscription trackers) solely for educational and analytical purposes. We are not a SEBI-registered investment advisor or stock broker. Users should conduct independent due diligence or consult a certified financial advisor before executing investment transactions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Updated versions will always be accessible from within the application and at this URL.
